TrinityMate privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

TrinityMate is a symptom tracker for MCAS, EDS and POTS. This policy covers the TrinityMate Android app and the TrinityMate web app.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we don't share it with anyone.

What the app stores

The app stores what you choose to enter, such as symptoms, triggers, foods, medicines, heart rate readings, joint events, notes and your profile details. It is saved only in the app's own storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. TrinityMate has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else, and it works without an internet connection.

Backups, exports and printing

You can save a backup file, export a CSV file, share a progress image or print a summary. These only happen when you tap the button, and you choose where the file goes, for example your Downloads folder, Google Drive or an email to your doctor. Once a file leaves the app, it's handled by whichever app or service you sent it to.

Android backup

If Android's own device backup is switched on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup, the same as for other apps. This is controlled by your phone, not by us, and you can switch it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It can vibrate the phone during the lean test, and it opens Android's own screens when you save, share or print.

Deleting your data

Tap the settings button in the app, then Erase all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything stored in it.

Children

TrinityMate is meant for adults and is not aimed at children.

Medical note

TrinityMate is for tracking and organising. It isn't medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
